Sequencing
BUILD TIMELINE

Products are built in reverse dependency order — starting with the most self-contained (ACT.live) and working back toward the data foundation. This means revenue from day one.

Phase 1 — Now (Weeks 1–6)
ACT.live — Complete MVP & First Client
Tasks 8–10 remaining (case intake, auth, WhatsApp). Deploy to Railway + Vercel. First client demo. First paying client at €10/unit/month. Self-contained — no dependency on DATA.live.
Phase 2 — Months 2–4
DATA.live + ALERT.live — Data Foundation
Build the scraping engine (Puppeteer + rotating residential proxies). Structure the Dutch property data pipeline. Deploy ALERT.live monitoring agent alongside it. Begin B2B data sales.
Phase 3 — Months 4–6
REPORT.live — Market Intelligence
AI-generated Dutch market reports powered by the DATA.live pipeline. B2B subscriptions at €99/month. Target: real estate investors, mortgage advisors, property agencies.
Phase 4 — Months 6–9
HOME.live — Consumer Platform
Expat property search. AI-powered scoring, instant alerts, English-language interface. Freemium model + mortgage referral fees. Requires full DATA.live pipeline to be live.
Phase 5 — Year 2
Scale & Enterprise
ACT.live enterprise tier for woningcorporaties. API integrations for property management software. Municipality (gemeente) notification automation — uncontested Dutch market feature. Combined platform positioning.

Operations
LEGAL STRUCTURE

All five products operate under a single registered business in the Netherlands.

Legal Entity
Mary's Eenmanszaak (KVK)
Registered sole trader in the Netherlands. Provides legal standing to contract with clients, issue invoices, and handle GDPR-regulated data. All five products registered under this entity.
Why This Structure
Secondary Activities Clause
The programme's co-founder (Nader) works at Linesight — a global construction consultancy — and cannot register a business in his own name due to an employment contract secondary activities clause. The Eenmanszaak under Mary resolves this cleanly.
Strategic Adjacency
Linesight — Real Estate & Construction
Nader's role at Linesight provides professional adjacency to real estate and construction — the primary client base for ACT.live and REPORT.live. Early client introductions without requiring a cold outreach network.
Compliance
GDPR-by-Design
All personal data stored in EU-hosted Supabase. AI generation receives minimal task-specific payloads only — no PII stored or processed by external AI. Security architecture is a client selling point, not a liability.
